# Papain Antibody Digestion Protocol

This protocol details the steps for digesting 1 mg of immunoglobulin G (IgG) antibody using papain. It ensures efficient generation of Fab and Fc fragments with precise reagent concentrations and reaction conditions.

---

## **Materials**

1. **Buffers and Reagents**
   - **1M Sodium Acetate, pH 5.5**
   - **IgG Antibody** (≥1 mg/mL)
   - **100 mM L-Cysteine** (prepared fresh)
   - **500 mM Iodoacetamide** (prepared fresh)
   - **Papain Enzyme** (~25 mg/mL)
   - **500 mM EDTA, pH ~7.5**

2. **Other Reagents**
   - **Glacial Acetic Acid**
   - **Distilled Deionized Water (ddH₂O)**

3. **Consumables**
   - **Microcentrifuge Tubes**
   - **Pipette Tips** (various sizes)
   - **Protein A or G Beads**
   - **30 kDa Molecular Weight Cut-Off (MWCO) Filters**

4. **Equipment**
   - **Pipettes and Sterile Pipette Tips**
   - **Incubator or Water Bath (37°C)**
   - **Refrigerator (4°C)**
   - **Centrifuge**
   - **Spectrophotometer (for A₍₂₈₀₎ measurements)**
   - **Magnetic Stirrer or Vortex Mixer**
   - **Calibrated pH Meter**

---

## **Protocol**

### **A. Activate Papain**

1. **Prepare Fresh 100 mM L-Cysteine Stock Solution**
   - **Weigh:** 6.05 mg of L-cysteine (MW 121.16 g/mol)
   - **Resuspend:** In **0.5 mL** of ddH₂O
   - **Note:** Prepare fresh to ensure activity.

2. **Prepare 1M Sodium Acetate, pH 5.5**
   - **Weigh:** 4.1 g of Sodium Acetate powder (MW 82.03 g/mol)
   - **Dissolve:** In **30 mL** of ddH₂O
   - **Adjust pH:** To 5.5 with glacial acetic acid
   - **Final Volume:** 50 mL with ddH₂O

3. **Prepare Papain Activation Reaction (200 µL for 10 mg IgG)**
   - **Components:**
     - **1M Sodium Acetate, pH 5.5:** 20 µL
     - **100 mM L-Cysteine:** 20 µL
     - **Papain (~25 mg/mL):** 20 µL
     - **500 mM EDTA, pH ~7.5:** 0.8 µL
     - **ddH₂O:** 139.2 µL
   - **Procedure:**
     1. In a sterile microcentrifuge tube, combine all components.
     2. Mix gently to ensure homogeneity.
   
4. **Incubate Papain Activation**
   - **Conditions:** 37°C for **20 minutes**

### **B. Digestion**

1. **Prepare Digestion Reaction (for 1 mg IgG at 1 mg/mL)**
   - **Components:**
     - **IgG Antibody (1 mg/mL):** 1 mL
     - **1M Sodium Acetate, pH 5.5:** 110 µL
     - **Papain Activation Reaction:** 20 µL
     - **500 mM EDTA, pH ~7.5:** 4 µL
   - **Procedure:**
     1. In a 1.5 ml tube, combine all components.
     2. Mix gently to ensure even distribution.

2. **Incubate for Digestion**
   - **Conditions:** 37°C for **3 to 5 hours**
   - **Optional:** Monitor progress via SDS-PAGE at 1-hour intervals.

### **C. Inactivation**

1. **Prepare Fresh 500 mM Iodoacetamide Stock Solution**
   - **Weigh:** 46 mg of iodoacetamide (MW 184.03 g/mol)
   - **Resuspend:** In **0.5 mL** of ddH₂O
   - **Note:** Prepare fresh to ensure reactivity.

2. **Add Iodoacetamide to Digestion Reaction**
   - **Volume:** **120 µL** of 500 mM iodoacetamide
   - **Final Concentration:** 50 mM
   - **Procedure:**
     1. Add **120 µL** of iodoacetamide to the digestion mixture.
     2. Mix thoroughly by gentle pipetting or vortexing.
     3. **Incubate:** At room temperature for **30 minutes**.

3. **Store Inactivated Digestion Reaction**
   - **Condition:** **4°C** until further use.

### **D. Purification**

1. **Remove Fc Fragments Using Protein A or G Beads**
   - **Procedure:**
     1. Add **20 µL** of Protein A or G beads (assuming 20mg/ml --> 0.4 mg of Fc) to the inactivated digestion reaction.
     2. **Incubate:** At **4°C** for **1 hour** to bind Fc fragments.
   
2. **Pellet Beads and Transfer Supernatant**
   - **Procedure:**
     1. Centrifuge at **10,000 × g** for **1 minute** to pellet the beads.
     2. Carefully transfer the **supernatant** to a new sterile microcentrifuge tube, leaving the beads behind.

3. **Filter and Concentrate Fab Fragments**
   - **Procedure:**
     1. Use centrifugal concentrator with **30 kDa MWCO filter** to remove Ionadacetamide. Wash with buffer of choice (HBS or PBS).
     2. **Quality Check:** Measure absorbance at **280 nm (A₍₂₈₀₎)** to verify removal of iodoacetamide and assess Fab concentration. Should be ~60% of the original IgG concentration.
     3. **Store:** Concentrated Fab fragments at **4°C**.
