Dawid has joined the [Department of Biomedical Informatics](https://medschool.cuanschutz.edu/dbmi) at the University of Colorado Anschutz as a secondary faculty member. The appointment strengthens the lab's computational side and opens new opportunities to collaborate with colleagues working at the intersection of data science, machine learning, and biomedicine.
