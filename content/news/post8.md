Stefano is joining the lab as a staff scientist. He comes to us from the Jensen Lab at Caltech and Brigham Young University, where he led cryo-ET research on bacterial molecular machines, from type IV pili to flagellar motors. He'll be leading our structural biology effort, with a particular focus on cryo-ET. Welcome aboard, Stefano!
